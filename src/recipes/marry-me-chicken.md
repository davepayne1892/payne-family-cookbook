The viral chicken dish with a terrible name.  It's a tomatoey update to creamy chicken.

### Ingredients
- Chicken breasts or breast fillets
- plain flour and paprika
- 1 large onion, chopped
- sun dried tomatoes or sun dried tomato paste
- 350 ml Chicken stock
- Dash of single cream 
- Fresh summer herbs like basil or thyme.

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 150 / 160 C.
1. Put some plain flour on a plate and stir through a little paprika.
1. Dredge the chicken through the flour mix to coat.
1. Fry off the chicken in a crock pot.
1. Add the diced onion and continue to fry until the chicken is browned and the onions translucent.
1. Add the tomatoes (chopped if not using paste).
1. Stir through, and then add the chicken stock.
1. Cover and add to oven for a couple of hours, stirring infrequently.
1. Add the cream, stir through and cook for a little longer,
1. Add the herbs if using and serve with carbs and green veg.
