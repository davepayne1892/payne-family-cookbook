Easy yorkshire pudding recipe which can be mixed ahead of time and kept in the fridge for later use. To ensure even cooking do not overcrowd the tray, cooking in batches if needed.

### Ingredients
- Three eggs (150ml ish)
- Equal amount semi skimmed milk
- Equal amount plain flour
- Salt, pepper and herbs of choice.

### Instructions
1. Find two cups of the same size, and crack the egg into one of them.
2. Measure the flour by pouring into the other cup and making sure the volumes match.
3. Pour the flour into a measuring jug, and refill the now empty cup with the semi skimmed milk and a small amount of water, so that this too is of same volume as the eggs.
4. Add the eggs to the flour and mix well. Season with salt pepper and herbs.
5. add a splash off the milk and mix. Add a slightly larger splash and mix again.
6. Continue adding the milk in larger increments, stirring each time to avoid any lumps or unmixed flour. Do this until all of the milk is used up or the mixture is becoming a runny batter.
7. Leave this mix as long as possible until required.
8. Preheat the oven as high as it will go. Oil a muffin tray and leave the tray in the oven until the oil is smoking. 
9. Pour the batter into the oiled muffin dips, add the tray back to the oven, lower temp to 220 / 230 C and cook for 20 minutes ish until dark brown.
