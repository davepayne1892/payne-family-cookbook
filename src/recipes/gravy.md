Slow cooked onion gravy which goes well with chicken, pork or sausage. Easily tweaked for other meats too.


### Ingredients
- Handful of onions
- Small amount of demerara sugar
- Fresh herbs of choice (sage, thyme, rosemary work well)
- 50g Plain flour
- 50g Butter
- 250ml to 1l Chicken stock 
- Cooking juices from meat
- White wine

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 160 C (or up to 180 if you need the onions to roast faster).
2. Chop the onions in half across the grain (so that one half has the root, and the other the stem). Leave the onions in their skins for now.
3. Shallow fry over a medium low heat until the exposed flesh is nearly sharred, and the rings appear to separate a little.
4. Put some foil in a baking tray, and add the onions flesh side up. Sprinkle on sugar, herbs and some oil.
5. Cover and cook for a couple of hours, the longer and slower the better.
6. Remove from the oven and allow to cool until you can handle the onions.
7. Remove the onion skins, stem and root. Blend the flesh until pureed. Add a little liquid (wine, stock or cooking juice to loosen if needed)
8. Create a roux in a large saucepan by melting the butted and then adding the flour. Stir constantly over a medium to low heat until the flour is cooked out.
9. Add the blended onion flesh, stir through and cook.
10. Treat the mix like a risooto, adding a small amount of liquid and cooking it down until it reduces and thickens. Add wine first, then stock, and finally the cooking juices.
11. The gravy can be ran through a sieve to make smoother, or frozen and reheated later.
