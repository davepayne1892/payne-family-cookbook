Decadent chocolate chip cookies made with nutty browned butter, crisp golden edges, and soft, chewy centres. Chilling the dough overnight allows the flavours to deepen and produces the perfect bakery-style texture.

### Ingredients
- 210g browned butter (from approximately one 250g block of unsalted butter)
- 300g plain flour
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 3/4 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp fine sea salt
- 140g light brown soft sugar
- 90g white granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 2 tsp vanilla bean paste
- 250g–300g chocolate chips or chopped chocolate

### Instructions
1. Brown a 250g block of unsalted butter in a saucepan over medium heat until the milk solids turn a deep golden brown with a nutty aroma.
2. Cool until it's opaque and soft, with the consistency of room-temperature butter.
3. Weigh out 210g of the browned butter and cream with both sugars for 2–3 minutes until lightened.
4. Beat in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla bean paste.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the plain flour, cornflour, bicarbonate of soda, baking powder, and salt. Fold into the wet ingredients until just combined.
6. Fold through the chocolate chips or chopped chocolate.
7. Portion into 80g balls, shaping them slightly taller than they are wide.
8. Chill overnight (or for at least 12 hours).
9. Bake straight from the fridge at 200°C (no fan) for around 12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centres are still soft.
10. Bang the tray on the worktop a couple of times and leave on the baking tray for 10 minutes after taking out of the oven.
