A timeless Italian classic that's easier to make than it looks. Serve with pasta, jacket potatoes, or nice bread.

### Ingredients
- Softrito mix (equal quantities or onion celery and carrot - diced).
- 100g pancetta or lardons, diced
- Dried herbs
- 500g Beef mince
- Bay leaf
- Tomoato puree
- Beef stock pot
- 2 Tins chopped tomatoes / peeled plum tomatoes etc.
- 50g Grana Padano cheese, freshly grated

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 150 / 160 C.
1. Shallow fry bacon lardons / pancetta in crock pot until a little crispy and oil has came out.
1. Add the sofrito mix, lower heat and cook until softened.
1. Add the herbs, stir through and then add the mince.
1. Brown the mince, and then add the tomato puree and stock pot.
1. Stir, add the two tins of tomatoes and then stir again.
1. Add the bay leaf, cover and add to the oven.
1. Cook for a couple of hours, stirring infrequently. Add the cheese, stir and cook for the final 15 mins or so.
